FONTLOG for Halden

Basic font information
----------------------
Halden is a modified version of Inter, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its proportions, widths and spacing were fitted to measurements of typefaces the requester chose. Every outline comes from the open base. No outline, point or kerning pair was taken from any other font.

Use: ui. Scripts: latin.

Files: ttf/Halden-Regular.ttf, webfonts/Halden-Regular.woff2, ttf/Halden-Medium.ttf, webfonts/Halden-Medium.woff2, ttf/Halden-SemiBold.ttf, webfonts/Halden-SemiBold.woff2, OFL.txt, FONTLOG.txt, fonts.css, README.md
