Anatomical precision

Every structural edit targets rapid visual recognition in automotive clusters, sidebars, and dense telemetry without adding decorative flair.

Single-story a and open apertures

Single-story a (cv11) and open-counter 3, 4, 6, and 9 (ss01) are baked directly into the default glyph slots, keeping small figures legible at a glance with no CSS feature flags required.

Continuous-curvature squircle micro-dots

Every dot contour uses a continuous superellipse profile rather than a rigid 90-degree square or playful circle, unifying punctuation with modern rounded HMI hardware and icons.

Restrained l-hook disambiguation

Lowercase l carries a tightened bottom hook pulled back by 12% in reach and 8% in bend height, separating cleanly from uppercase I and numeral 1 without disrupting dense words like Dashline or all.

Contextual optical centering for telemetry

GPOS calt rules lift colons, hyphens, en-dashes, and multiply signs into the optical center whenever flanked by digits, keeping clocks, ranges, and resolutions balanced automatically.

Optical sizes and zero-reflow OLED Dark

Bright white glyphs bloom optically on dark OLED dashboards. Halden ships a dedicated Dark grade thinned by 5% with advance widths locked unit-for-unit to Light Text so theme transitions never shift a single pixel.
